# Priority papers for the survey

| Survey question | Papers to compare | What to extract |
|---|---|---|
| Does apologizing concede the factual point? | [Epistemic authority after disagreement](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1595/) and [SaFeRDialogues](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.447/) | Code apology, acknowledgement, factual stance and evidential support independently. In the first study, 59% of apologies accompanied retention of the original claim. The second tests how people judge a response to feedback after a safety failure. |
| What does “I understand” accomplish? | [Grounding Gaps](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.348/) and [multi-turn repair](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.651/) | Identify what was acknowledged, who initiated repair, and whether the next turn demonstrates understanding. |
| When should a model change its answer? | [Towards Understanding Sycophancy](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/0105f7972202c1d4fb817da9f21a9663-Abstract-Conference.html), [Ask Again, Then Fail](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.577/) and [balanced persuasion](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.412/) | Cross initial correctness with the quality of the challenge. Measure correct-to-wrong and wrong-to-correct transitions with their own denominators. |
| Does a visual answer follow the image or the hint? | [Pointing to a Llama](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1020/) and [Exploring Response Uncertainty](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.916/) | Check that the model can identify the relevant feature on the same image before it receives a misleading cue. |
| When is peer consensus warranted? | [BenchForm](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/1da9ca7e9cef4b1af63913f05d1630a4-Abstract-Conference.html), [When Identity Skews Debate](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.650/) and [Debate-aware Learning](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1907/) | Track private initial answers, peer identity, majority position, argument quality and the final answer. |

Reddy, Ming Jin, Lifu Huang | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.661.pdf) | Reduced agreement with bad hints leaves a separate question about receptiveness to good evidence. |
| EMNLP 2025 | [Exploring Response Uncertainty in MLLMs: An Empirical Evaluation under Misleading Scenarios](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.916/)<br>Yunkai Dang, Mengxi Gao, Yibo Yan, Xin Zou, Yanggan Gu, Jungang Li, Jingyu Wang, Peijie Jiang, Aiwei Liu, Jia Liu, Xuming Hu | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.916.pdf) | The flip rate is conditional on an initially correct answer. | ACL 2026 | [Narrative License and Model Sycophancy in LLM Summaries of Scientific Work](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.746/)<br>Calvin Isch, Grace Jennings | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.746.pdf) | A summary can amplify a claim to please a reader without reversing a discrete answer. |
| ACL 2026 | [Too Nice to Tell the Truth: Quantifying Agreeableness-Driven Sycophancy in Role-Playing Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1421/)<br>Arya Shah, Deepali Mishra, Chaklam Silpasuwanchai | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1421.pdf) | The manipulation concerns role-playing personas. Polite wording alone cannot establish a factual concession. |
| ACL 2026 | [Feeling Right vs. Being Right: How AI Sycophancy Affects Value-Laden Deliberation](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2046/)<br>Jeongwoo Ryu, Soomin Kim, Jinsu Eun, Kyusik Kim, Changhoon Oh, Bongwon Suh | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2046.pdf) | The study measures short-term human outcomes on value-laden dilemmas. It cannot establish long-term retention. |
| EMNLP 2026 | [How Identity and Opinion Shape Political Sycophancy in LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1500/)<br>Li-Ni Fu, Chang Chih Meng, Chien-Hua Chen, Hen-Hsen Huang, I-Chen Wu | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1500.pdf) | Score movement relative to the model’s baseline stance. Many items have no single correct political position. |
| EMNLP 2026 | [Unmasking Superficial Honesty: Mechanistic Head-Driven Activation Steering for Sycophancy-Free Medical VLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1732/)<br>Mengliang Zhang, Prajwal Challa, Wentao Gao | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1732.pdf) | Report visual grounding and answer flips separately. | EMNLP 2026 | [The Consistency Illusion: How Multi-Agent Debate Hides Reasoning Misalignment](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1780/)<br>Xiaoyang Wang, Christopher Yang | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1780.pdf) | Agreement on an answer can conceal different rationales. Rationale similarity is also an imperfect proxy for accuracy. |
| EMNLP 2026 | [Debate-aware Learning Makes LLMs Genuine Debaters](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1907/)<br>Yufei Zhang, Kai Xiong, Yuxiong Yan, Bibo Cai, Xiao Ding, Bing Qin, Ting Liu | [Publisher PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1907.pdf) | Responding to an argument and accepting its conclusion are distinct outcomes. Record how argument strength is scored. |
| ICLR 2025 | [Do as We Do, Not as You Think: the Conformity of Large Language Models](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/1da9ca7e9cef4b1af63913f05d1630a4-Abstract-Conference.html)<br>Zhiyuan Weng, Guikun Chen, Wenguan Wang | [Publisher PDF](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/1da9ca7e9cef4b1af63913f05d1630a4-Paper-Conference.pdf) | Read conformity alongside correctness.
